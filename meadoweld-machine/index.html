The O'Bear Rail Saw

Our rail saws are designed to maximize productivity on any rail maintenance activity requiring rail cutting. O'Bear Rail Saws are air-cooled, gas or diesel powered, and make burr-free cuts on either side of the track — without moving the machine.

  • Patented oscillating system reduces the arc of contact between wheel and rail
  • Pendant-controlled power feed improves blade life and reduces operator fatigue
  • Standard safety features and heavy-duty rail clamps
  • Improved spindle design, spark arrestor, lifting bale, parking brake, and blade storage
  • Power feed and self-propelling models available
  • Leasing and rentals available
